This page explains how storage and tracking technologies are used on Garcia Builder Fitness pages, including the starter assessment flow.

Essential and Security Storage

These settings help keep the site secure, preserve core functionality and maintain service reliability. They are enabled by default.

Functionality Storage

Used to remember preferences such as language and interface state that improve usability.

Analytics Storage

Used to understand page usage and funnel performance through analytics tools. This storage respects your cookie preference selection.

Advertising Storage, User Data and Personalization

Used for advertising measurement and campaign optimization where consent allows. Consent Mode signals include:

  • ad_storage
  • ad_user_data
  • ad_personalization

How to Reject or Change Choices

You can reject non-essential categories and reopen cookie preferences at any time.
