Privacy Policy

Revision date: 2026-07-27

This Privacy Policy explains how Garcia Builder Fitness collects, uses and protects personal information submitted through the website, including the starter assessment flow.

Information We Collect

  • Starter assessment responses (goal, routine and support preference)
  • Contact information such as first name, email and optional WhatsApp number
  • Consent records for resource delivery and optional marketing communication
  • Attribution details such as UTM parameters, landing path and click identifiers
  • Payment and subscription metadata for paid services processed by Stripe

How We Use Information

  • To generate and deliver requested starter-plan results and resources
  • To respond to enquiries and coaching-related requests
  • To operate analytics and advertising measurement where consent allows
  • To process subscriptions and purchases
  • To improve platform performance and reliability

Processors and Services

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers required to operate the service, including Supabase (data storage), Zapier (automation), transactional email providers (Brevo or SMTP provider), Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Pixel and Stripe.

Consent and Marketing

Resource delivery consent and marketing consent are separate. Marketing email and WhatsApp consent are optional and can be withdrawn. Cookie preferences can be changed at any time.

Retention and Security

We retain data only for operational and legal purposes and apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No internet transmission or storage is fully risk free.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent by contacting andre@garciabuilder.fitness.
